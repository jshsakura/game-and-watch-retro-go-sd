# 32X PCM span — 2026-10-05

Kolibri's forest scene improves from **16.3079 FPS to 17.0412 FPS (+4.4968%)**
with a bounded native SH-2 sample/position span. This is a measured hardware
result against main commit `33b49a68`, picodrive `ce4ebb6c`. The source is on
the branch `perf/32x-pcm-span-20261005`. The initial experiment restored the
previous firmware; the user subsequently requested deployment. The release
integration includes current `testbed` fixes and uses the normal release
configuration, without finite benchmark stops, forced autoboot, disabled
idle shutdown, or disabled boot rescue. Deployment evidence is recorded
under `build/32x-continue-20261004/deploy/` in the original workspace.

## Change

The MULS handler recognizes opcode `230f` and validates the live SDRAM routine,
then replaces the multiply and forward/reverse sample-position updates with
equivalent host operations. Recognition uses current opcodes and safe memory
ranges, without a ROM title, hash or fixed guest address. The span preserves
registers, MACL, PC/PPC, EA, T and interpreter cycle charges. Its fractional
position write uses the original bus accessor. IRQ, delay-slot, insufficient
budget, alignment, opcode and code/voice overlap guards decline unchanged.

The helper lives in XIP; the measured candidate leaves **40 bytes of ITCM**.
The finite measurement build leaves 172 bytes of RAM_EMU. Production adds no
counter BSS. `GNW_NO_PCM_SPAN` disables the new handler. Synthetic tests and
live context comparison are enabled only by `PCM_SPAN_SHADOW_PROOF`;
`RIG_SH2_COUNT` enables invocation counters.

## Hardware evidence

Two predetermined A/B/A rounds used cold boot, no savestate or input,
volume 1, 340 MHz, warmup 2,200 frames and an identical 400-frame window.
Each sample completed and drew all 400 frames; profiling was disabled.
Resident GDB completion stops supplied timestamps and captures, without
progress polling. The first sample's external timer agreed within 100 ms.

| Round | A before | B | A after | Gain | A drift |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 16.30656 | 17.04158 | 16.30856 | +4.5011% | +0.01223% |
| 2 | 16.30856 | 17.04086 | 16.30789 | +4.4924% | -0.00408% |

Mean A (n=4): 16.30789 FPS; mean B (n=2): 17.04122 FPS. These are descriptive
samples for this specific scene, rather than statistical significance or a
claim about all games. All six endpoint framebuffers match SHA-256
`24a17a326a64544d24729a349c71d59186718932e3389df2dd4eb064b457bfe1`.
The captured screen shows forest gameplay, with the bird and enemies.

The hardware's endpoint sound buffer is zero after DMA stops. It therefore
does not establish physical audio equality. All samples had equal audio work
and stopped DMA; correctness evidence for audio is the rig's whole-run hash,
sample count and energy signatures below. Pixel dumps are RGB565.

The original host supervisor exited with code 143 after the sixth GDB
completion and all binary dumps. The final report was reconstructed offline
from those artifacts after checking completion, record length and all flashed
artifact readbacks. No sample was rerun. Original bank2, core, XIP, CONFIG,
off.sav and selector were subsequently restored, SHA-256 readbacks matched
the backups, and normal bank2 was started.

## Correctness and rig cost

Synthetic comparison against the real interpreter passed 1,824 complete
6,016-byte contexts, both directions and both branch outcomes. Another
10,464 guarded declines left context and the touched memory unchanged.
4,096 live comparisons also passed. The final reviewed source repeated this
proof successfully through a complete 600-frame replay. GNW builds with
the handler enabled and disabled compile successfully. Generic/no-fastloops
builds remain unsupported by the existing baseline native kernels.

| Rig workload | Frames/window | M7 instructions/frame A → B | Cost change |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Kolibri | 2600 / 2200–2600 | 17,390,105 → 16,707,330 | -3.9262% |
| Doom | 1800 / 1200–1800 | 11,396,893 → 11,400,602 | +0.0325% |
| After Burner | 1800 / 1200–1800 | 15,722,903 → 15,739,666 | +0.1066% |

All three workloads have equal complete endpoint dumps, sampled frame hashes,
whole-run audio hashes, sample counts and energy signatures. Kolibri's audio
hash is `e238f9d9`, over 3,822,000 samples. These QEMU costs are separate from
hardware FPS; Doom and After Burner have no new hardware speed claim.

## Artifacts and next hypothesis

Evidence is preserved in the original workspace under
`build/32x-continue-20261004/`: `device/result.json`, two round comparisons,
immutable `device-arms`, backup/restore manifests, `proof-review-source`,
`compile-modes`, and `case-*-v3/result.json`. ROMs are user-owned inputs and
are not included in the source change. To exercise the proof with the normal
rig, set `EXTRA_DEF=-DPCM_SPAN_SHADOW_PROOF` and run `run_32x.sh` for 600
Kolibri frames in an environment with the ARM compiler and host QEMU.

Experimental v5 combines this PCM span with a table-search backedge fold.
Its rig cost reductions are 7.3583% (Kolibri), 4.4129% (After Burner) and
1.4202% (Doom), with equal sound/screen signatures. Its bounded live proof
passed 4,096 contexts, but the long proof replay hit its finite timeout under
concurrent QEMU load. It has no production-link or hardware validation and
is excluded from this source change. Its snapshots and partial proof remain
in the evidence directory for a separate experiment.
